The run. GreyboxCorridor in Play, ground ShelledForest (the scene's own default), NightMud look, rain forced to 0,
sky pinned (Atmosphere.PinnedClock = 120), sim frozen (SimHost.TimeScale = 0) so no man moves or wades, presentation

Why every moment has its own reference and the plate is only for the record: the water's foam and lapping are
animated from _Time.y in TWWater.hlsl, so a still taken a second after the plate differs from it across the whole
water sheet - about 1,600 changed pixels in a 3 m disc at the landing point, whether anything landed there or not.
Measured that way the first run of this capture read ring_px_pre 1577 against ring_px_land 1703 and could not tell
a splash from the river moving. A reference rendered at the same frozen instant with the splash lifted out has the
same foam, the same body, the same shadows and the same sky.

Two things seen in these frames that are not the claim under test, recorded because they are visible:
 - The ring itself does not read at T1. A man's ring is 1.8 m wide and opens from a point, so 0.03 s after the
   landing it is a 0.3 m circle about two pixels thick on dark night water. Lifting the rings out ALONE moved no
   pixel by more than 0.014 luma against a 0.011 noise floor, while lifting the Splash card out as well moved 326
   pixels by up to 0.108. What a man's water entry shows at this view is the card; the ring is detail under it.
 - The body is gone from view at land + 0.033 s: it is under the surface, not floating or lying in the shallows.
