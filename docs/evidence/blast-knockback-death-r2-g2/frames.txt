What this set says, against the goal's three findings:
  - "the after frame drops the soldier mesh (drawn infantry 0, vertices 0, men in frame 0)": the living counters are
    still 0 in a +3 s frame, and that is correct -- a dead man is out of the sim world, so there is nothing living
    left to count. What the frame draws is now counted separately: drawn_fallen is 5-6 in every blast run's +3 s
    still, with fallen_vertices to match. The -1 s stills read men_in_frame 6 and contrast_median 0.20, where round 2
    read men_in_frame 0: a man seen from straight above is sized across the screen now, not head to foot.
  - "blast3's row becomes black speckles": the speckles were the measurement, not the picture. blast3's patch borders
    a frozen pond and a ruined wall, and round 2's median-colour background made all of it foreground. Against its
    own plate that clutter subtracts out and blast3 matches 4 bodies out of 30 candidates.
  - "put the nearest-body bar outside the spread": done, above.
