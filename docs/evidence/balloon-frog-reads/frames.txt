WHAT THE PIXELS SAY (probe-before.json, written by probe.py beside these files)
  0 of 8 tiles read the sac. Every tile: blob 0 px, so no contrast and no width could be measured.
  The sac is not small-and-low-contrast at zoom 30 — in the shipping Night look it is not pale at all:
    - the brightest pixel in the whole of t1 has Rec.709 linear luma 0.1304 (rgb 85,101,137 — a rain streak);
    - the probe's pale-sac floor is 0.45 linear, which nothing in a Night frame can reach;
    - 11 pixels out of 1 440 000 are greener than they are blue. #e9f0c8 does not survive the night grade.
  The whole frame's luma_mean is 0.214 and p95 0.293 (CaptureRig's 0-1 Rec.709 scale). CaptureRig's own sidecar
  warns on these frames: "men barely separate from the ground: unreadable".

  So the critic's "~20 px pale blob at zoom 30" was generous. At the shipping look and band there is no pale blob:
  the frogs are ~15 px dark silhouettes against dark mud, sac and all. Whether the fix may touch the night grade
  or must stay inside the frog's own art is an owner decision — see docs/reference/decisions.md, the open row
  "the balloon frog's sac at zoom 30 in the Night look".
