Getting even two is the finding: DeathGag.Planted is a coin flip (DeathGags.PlantShare = 0.5) among throws over
PlantMinHigh = 5 m that are not a heap and not in a trench, and a heap (two or more deaths within a tick's
PileRadius) forces Fountain instead -- so every retry near x ~89.5, where the sim always parks the dead, piles onto
the last corpse and locks the gag off for the rest of that play session. Dozens of single-man shells across several
fresh play sessions produced exactly two Planted deaths that landed on ground above the water line; a third was not
found inside the time this leg could spend driving the editor by hand. See note.md for the budget this cost and
what the next leg should do differently (a scripted multi-session driver, not an interactive retry loop).
