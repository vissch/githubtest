water-entry-splash-r8 - the baseline round (the UNCHANGED build), frame by frame.

Every JPG under base/ is for the eye. Every number in probe.json was read off the PNGs in the
git-ignored trench-warfare-3d/Captures/water-entry-r8/run-N/, never off a JPG: JPEG chroma
subsampling moves luma by more than the 0.02 threshold the probe counts with, so a measurement
taken on one of these pictures would be a measurement of the compressor.
