THE AT RIFLEMAN, RE-STAGED WITH NO FIRE IN THE FRAME        (2026-10-27, atrifle-restage-no-fire-2)
====================================================

WHY THIS SET EXISTS
-------------------
The owner's answer on the Decide page to "The AT rifleman's readability shot: clear the fire, or move the camera
in" was: re-stage with no fire in the frame. r3-g1 measured the prone AT rifleman at 0.7655 against the Sniper,
but the fires owned the largest moving blob in every plate and their motion sat inside the masks. This set is
r3-g1 again, one camera, one place, the same two men - with the fires taken out of the world instead of out of
the arithmetic.


WHAT CHANGED FROM r3-g1, AND ONLY THAT
--------------------------------------
The fires. The editor was started with

  TW_KNOBS=lights.maxFires=0,lights.maxTorches=0,look.moreFires=0

in its environment. That is the right lever and not Knobs.Set: Knobs.ResetSession drops every override when Play
ends and re-reads the environment, and NightLights reads these three once in Start. Lanterns and trench lamps are
lamps, not fire, and stay lit; the frame did not go dark enough to trip the readability warning - every one of
the fifteen sidecars reads warnings [].

Everything else is r3-g1 line for line: scene GreyboxCorridor, VfxLab.Rain(0), the AT rifleman at (50, 25) and
the Sniper at (45, 25), Hp pinned on every write, CaptureRig.Rig.YawPin = 0, Hold(120) before every shot,
Release() after, and the SAME camera for all fifteen stills - focus (47.5, 25), zoom 30, yaw 180, pitch 22,
1600x900. Three rounds of five, three live poses.


THE FRAME SAYS IT ITSELF
------------------------
No flame was counted by eye. Every still carries flames_in_frame, which CaptureRig works out from the capture's
own matrices over every flame foot NightLights placed, the small torches included, and the three knobs beside
it. All fifteen read:

  flames_in_frame 0   knob_maxFires 0   knob_maxTorches 0   knob_moreFires 0

probe.py in this folder is r3-g1's probe with one gate added: a sidecar that does not read 0 on all four fails
the set outright (exit 2). The rest of its gates are untouched, so the two readings are comparable.

CombatFx still lights muzzle flashes and burning wrecks at run time, so flames_in_frame speaks for the static
flame meshes only. Nobody is shooting in these stills and nothing burns in them: the count and the picture agree.


THE STAGING, AND THE SIX THINGS r3-g1'S RECIPE DID NOT SURVIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------
Each one was found by a probe failure on this battle, not guessed at. The driver is rounds.py in the leg folder,
every eval line and its answer in evals.log there.

1. THE SLOT IS NOT FIXED. TankCapture.Spawn handed back slot 12 / slot 0 on one run and slot 4 / slot 12 on the
   next. r3-g1's hard-coded 12 and 0 staged the wrong men. The driver now reads the slot out of Spawn's answer.

2. A PLATE MUST HOLD NOBODY, AND THE CIRCLE WAS TOO SMALL. r3-g1 cleared alive men within 120 m, once, and moved
   0. This battle walked a man into floor-2 at that radius. The clearing now reaches 400 m - as far as the
   presenter draws - and runs before every write, not once.

3. A CORPSE IS DRAWN TOO. r3-g1's clearing skipped Hp <= 0. The plates that still failed held a fallen man, not
   a walker. The clearing takes every infantry slot, alive or dead; only the position moves, Hp is untouched.

4. THE CLEARING AND THE FREEZE MUST BE ONE EVAL. As two, the sim ran on between them and spawned a man into
   floor-1. One eval now sweeps and sets TimeScale=0 together.

Two more, about the pose rather than the frame:

5. THE PHASE AT THE SHOT IS NOT THE PHASE YOU ASKED FOR. r3-g1's fixed spells (0.4 / 1.2 / 2.0 s) gave 0.60,
   0.91, 0.61 on this battle - one pose, and two pixel-identical AT masks. The staging writes themselves run the
   sim on. The driver now nudges and re-stages until the prone man's phase is 0.12 clear of every phase already
   shot, and it reads the phase back AFTER the capture to prove it did not drift: 0.72 -> 0.72, 0.93 -> 0.93,
   0.20 -> 0.20.

6. A MAN WRITTEN HOME SPENDS A MOMENT GETTING UP. A Sniper still caught in that moment is a different man: one
   such still alone pushed the spread to 0.8058 and sank the set. The driver now waits for his post's own idle
   clip before his still. Round 1 settled on KneelAimedIdle, rounds 2 and 3 on KneelIdle.

THE READING
-----------
  python docs/evidence/atrifle-restage-no-fire-2/probe.py   ->  exit 0
  verdict: the prone AT rifleman reads as his own man

  silhouette_diff       0.6019     (the three readings: 0.7715, 0.6019, 0.6701 - the worst is quoted)
  the bar               0.6
  spread_at             0.1047
  spread_sniper         0.4701
  spread_max            0.4701     (must stay under 0.6)
  at_phases             0.72, 0.93, 0.20     gap at least 0.21
  px_per_m              25.001     disagreement between the two men 0.0004

0.6019 against r3-g1's 0.7655. The same two men, the same camera, the same bar: taking the fire out of the frame
costs 0.16 of the reading, and it clears 0.6 by 0.002. That is the honest number and it is not rounded up. The
bar was not moved to fit it, and the margin is thin enough that the next re-stage of this pair should expect to
land either side of it.

Why it falls: in r3-g1 the firelight raked the prone man from the side and gave his silhouette edges the plate
did not have. Without it he is lit by lanterns and sky alone, and he reads less distinct against the Sniper -
while still reading as his own man. The spread is wider too (0.4701 against r3-g1's 0.2655), carried entirely by
the Sniper: his kneeling idle changes his outline more from round to round than the prone man's does.
