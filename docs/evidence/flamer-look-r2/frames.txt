flamer-look-r2 — what each still is, and how it was made (2026-10-19, before the fix)

Scene      GreyboxCorridor, in Play, batch-mode editor of the checkout githubtest-relay-work2.
           The scene is already Mood.Night / Biome.NightMud — the owner's target look. No knob was touched.
Spot       x 42, z 12. Map.Height is 2.5 m and level over x 30..48, z 10..16: flat open mud, a bunker behind.
Camera     yaw 90 (TacticalCamera.BaseYaw is -90, so the camera looks along +z, which is the way these men are
           drawn: we stand behind him, where the fuel tanks are). pose_error_m is 0.000 on all seven stills.
Moment     every still is a frozen world: SimHost.TimeScale = 0 and CaptureRig.Hold(120) — weather clock held at
           120 s, rain 0.20, wind 3.0, identical in all seven. The man was placed, moved onto the spot, given a
           few frames to be seen by the controller, then frozen. Nothing moves between the frames of a band.
One man per frame, not two side by side: see the WHY block at the top of probe.py. Two men in one frame put two
different pieces of background in the two boxes and the control came out at 7075 px against a cap of 10.

  flamer_t3.png    T3 (zoom 7.5, pitch 13)  one Flamethrower (archetype 36) alone on the spot, from behind.
  flamer_t2.png    T2 (zoom 16,  pitch 22)  the same man, same frozen world, the standard fighting view.
  flamer_t1.png    T1 (zoom 30,  pitch 25)  the same man, the far view.
  assault_t3.png   T3  an Assault (archetype 1) standing on the same spot, same camera, same held clock.
  assault_t2.png   T2  the same.
  assault_t1.png   T1  the same.
  control_t3.png   T3  a SECOND Assault on the spot: the control. Against assault_t3 it must read ~0 differing
                       pixels, and it reads exactly 0 — the method has no noise of its own.

  *_boxes.png      the same stills with the projected boxes drawn on them (quantised to 128 colours), so a human
                   can see the boxes are on the man: red tanks, orange wand, yellow the whole kit, blue torso,
                   green the ground sample, white cross his feet.

What they show (probe.json, measured off these very files after they were resampled to 1280x720)
  kit_px_t3            278  (needs >= 150)  the kit IS drawn at T3 — 278 of 153x56 px differ from the bare man
  kit_px_t2            142  (needs >= 40)   and at T2
  kit_px_control         0  (needs <= 10)   the method measures nothing when there is no kit
  tank_vs_ground_dL_t2  12.78 (needs >= 10) the tanks are a little lighter than the mud he stands on
  tank_vs_torso_dL_t2   -6.64 (needs >= 12) FAIL — the tanks are DARKER than his own torso. This is the critic's
                       second finding in a number: the kit is drawn in dirtMat, dirt on dirt, at night.
  (t1 is measured too and is not a threshold: 4 px of 37x14 differ — at the far view the kit is gone.)
