-------------------------

* `at_rifles` (the capture's `CombatFx.AtRiflesDrawn`, the guns of the last frame, added to the
  sidecar in commit b7d5b9a1bc) must be 1 in each `pair-{r}` and 0 in all six plates;
* `at_area_m2` and `at_span_m` are reported per round, and `AT_MIN_AREA_M2` 0.7 m2 is a gate. A

  prone man plus a 1.65 m gun is about 2.5 m ahead of them, so the old box would cut him. A mask
  that touches its own box edge now fails too - a truncated mask is a reading of the box;
