Five rocketed men planted head first in the mud (DeathGag.Planted), one man per shell, five shells, in the battle
scene GreyboxCorridor at the standard zoom (T1: zoom 30, yaw +21, pitch 25, 1600x900). Each is taken one second
after that man lands, while his legs are still kicking (the kick runs three seconds, then they fold).
Each shot is aimed at ground level under the man (aimY = RenderGround.Sample at his resting x z), so the frame
centre is where his head went in and his legs stand 0.905 m above it. The clock is NOT held: the editor is paused
and stepped (Tools/tw pause true, stepto), so game time stands still between the step and the shot.
The men come to rest where the sim parks them, around x 89.5; the five z are different places along that line.
Only ground above the water line was used: the shell holes there are flooded, and a body under water shows
nothing of any death, let alone this one.
