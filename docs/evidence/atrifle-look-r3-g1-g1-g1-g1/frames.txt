atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g1-g1 — the frame in which the rifle reads
================================================================

Why there is a round g1-g1-g1-g1
-------------------------------
The owner sent back atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g1, which passed its own bar: "Frame the prone AT
rifleman so the rifle reads at zoom 30. The mask is 428, 446 and 443 pixels, and the three pair
frames are the night field with a speck behind the bags. Bar outside the spread of three captures
of the current build."

That set's own frames.txt admits the fault in one line: "At zoom 30 the 1.65 m anti-tank rifle is a
few dark pixels. No number here isolates it." It was shot 1600x900, its AT masks were 428-446 px,
and its capture recorded no gun at all — so nothing in it could have isolated the rifle even in
principle. This round changes the frame and adds the two numbers that measure the gun.

The arithmetic the frame rests on
---------------------------------
At zoom 30 the rig's framing is FIXED. `CaptureRig` sets `distance = Zoom*tan30/tan(fov/2)` with
fov 25 (CloseZoom is 15), so the half-height it frames on the ground is always `Zoom*tan30` =
17.32 m and

    px_per_m (on the ground) = HPX / 34.64

whatever the pitch, the yaw or the focus. H 900 gave the old set 25.0 px/m and a 41 px rifle. Pitch,
yaw and focus change only WHERE the camera looks, never the scale. So the frame is the only lever.

The gun is our own prop, not part of the baked figure: `CombatFx.AtRifle.cs`, `AtRifleLength`
1.65 m, laid by `CombatFx.Lay`. Its barrel box is 0.035 half-extent — 0.07 m thick, which is 0.042
of the gun's length, a scale-free ratio. A gun drawn 100 px long therefore has a 4.2 px barrel, and
that is where the probe's `MIN_GUN_LEN_PX` 100 comes from.

    frame            1100 x 2340       67.6 px per ground metre      ~2.6 MB a still, 39 MB the set
    the rifle        123.5 px          its barrel 5.2 px             three times the old set's 41

A taller frame cannot be bought. `men_in_frame` skips a man nearer an edge than his own `tall_px`
(CaptureRig ~334) and the two men stand 4.5 m either side of the focus, so the width has to grow
with the height: `W >= 2*(tall_px + 5.5 m * px_per_m)`. At 1100x2340 that is 2*(133 + 5.5*67.6) =
1010 against W 1100 — already at the limit. H 3280, the height a 150 px gun would need, would want
W 1424, ~6.5 MB a still and ~98 MB for the set, in a repo with no LFS. H could not be shrunk below
2340 either: at 1800 the gun falls to ~95 px, under the gate.

(`px_per_m` in probe.json is 50.9, a different number for a different thing: it is `tall_px / 2.6 m`,
so it measures a man standing UP, and at pitch 45 a vertical metre projects to fewer pixels than a
metre laid flat. The gun lies flat, so the ground scale is the one that governs it.)

The pitch, measured and not guessed
-----------------------------------
Four pitches at yaw 180, the same staging, each an AT-only still against its own plate, the gun
segment the capture itself recorded sampled against his silhouette (200 points):

    pitch   gun_len_px   gun_on_mask   contrast_median   the capture's own warning
      22      117.7         0.405         0.827          —
      35      120.9         0.620         0.111          men barely separate from the ground
      45      123.5         0.680         0.986          —
      55      126.1         0.615         0.422          —

Pitch 22 — the previous set's pitch — is the one that fails: from there the sandbags cut the gun and
only 40 % of it lands on the man. Pitch 35 clears the gun gates and fails the picture; the capture
calls the man unreadable against the ground at contrast 0.111, under its own 0.12 bar. Pitch 55
clears everything but reads less of the gun than 45. **Pitch 45 is the smallest pitch that clears
every rule**, and it is the best of the four on both the gun and the contrast. The gun grows with
the pitch because the rifle lies flat beside a prone man: the steeper the camera, the less of its
length is foreshortened.

One number in the plan could not be met and was not faked: it asked the scout to pick the smallest
pitch with a gun **150 px** long. At 1100x2340 the ceiling is ~126 px and a 150 px gun needs H 3280,
which breaks the width rule above and triples the git weight. The plan's own probe gate is 100 px,
the measured gun is 123.5, and that is what is claimed here.

The staging
-----------
Unchanged from r3-g1-g1-g1 except the camera. One camera for all fifteen stills: focus (47.5, 25),
zoom 30 (T1, the owner's number), yaw 180 with `CaptureRig.Rig.YawPin = 0`, **pitch 45**,
**1100x2340**. Rain 0. Seed 12648430, battlefield seed 1917, ground ShelledForest, bombardment
0/min.

    who          archetype   staged at        what he does
    AT rifleman  27          (50.0, 25.0)     prone on the surface, behind the parapet
    Sniper        3          (43.0, 26.0)     a trench post, kneeling (one post off the brazier)
    everyone else —          (-5000, -5000)   cleared before every round, and again before a still
                                              the capture counted the wrong number of men in

Each round: clear the field, park both, shoot `empty`; stage both, settle out the JumpDown that a
staging write plays, shoot `pair`; park the Sniper, drift the AT man to his phase, shoot `at`; park
the AT man, stage the Sniper back, settle, wait out `TrenchRoutine` until it plays this round's
kneel, shoot `sniper`; park him and shoot `floor`. Nothing is written to the world during a drift.
`empty` and `floor` are the two man-less plates of the same frame; what differs between them is
weather, and the probe subtracts it before it labels a blob.

Which three kneels, and why these three
---------------------------------------
    round 1   KneelIdle         his base kneel at the post (1.7 s loop)
    round 2   FidgetRubEyes     the third fidget of the routine's beat (1.8 s once-clip, Crouch)
    round 3   KneelLookAround   the kneeling fidget: same legs, different arms (5.3 s once-clip)

Round 2 was `StoopIdle` last round and the new pitch killed it: looking down at 45 degrees the squat
and the kneel read almost alike, and the pair spread **0.2444** and then **0.2331** at two different
phases — under the 0.25 bar both times, so it is the angle and not the moment. `KneelInspect`, the
plan's named fallback, went too far the other way: **0.6345** against KneelIdle and **0.6965**
against KneelLookAround, which would have put the Sniper's own self-spread OVER the 0.6 bar and made
the reading meaningless. `FidgetRubEyes` (`AnimationController` ~880, the same `pick >= 0.84` beat
that gives KneelInspect and KneelLookAround) sits between them. **No bar was moved to get there.**

Rounds 1 and 2 were each re-shot. Round 1's first take caught a weather event between its two

PASS_DIFF 0.6, unchanged since 13d1158177, as are MIN_POSE_SPREAD 0.25, MIN_NOISE_RATIO 10,
PHASE_BAND, AT_CLIP, MIN_PHASE_GAP 0.2, HEADING_TOL, DOMINANCE, MIN_AREA_M2 and MAX_BOX_SHARE. The
claim is made on the WORST round: 0.6305 against a self-spread of 0.4389, so the bar sits outside
the worse self-spread of three captures of the current build, which is what the owner asked for.

`BOX_DOWN` was raised from 0.2 to 0.6 of `tall_px`, and that is the one probe constant that moved.
A prone man's gun lies on the ground BELOW the point the capture calls his feet — the box is
projected from his drawn position, and 2.6 m of man laid flat reaches well under it — so at 0.2 the
lower third of the rifle fell outside his own box and was dropped from his mask. 0.6 was measured:
it is what it takes for the whole laid gun to sit inside the box at pitch 45.

The honest limits
-----------------
* The gun measure says the rifle was DRAWN 123.5 px long and that 62-74 % of it falls on the man's
  own silhouette. It does not say a player picks it out. Nobody has looked at these crops yet.
* The parapet still halves the prone man, and the two men face different ways: one camera yaw cannot
  be side-on to both, so `heading_diff_rad` is 1.11 in all three rounds. It is reported and NOT
  gated — one frame was the owner's call. What IS gated is each man against himself across the
  stills of a round, and that is 0.00 rad everywhere.
* One seed, one night, one ground, one camera, and now one pitch as well. Nothing here says the
  rifle reads from another angle, at another time of day, or in rain.
* Round 2's `at` still has 6611 px of weather inside the AT man's box, the most of any still here.
  It is subtracted before the mask is labelled, and his mask still comes out 4499 px with a 391 px
  second blob, but round 2 is the noisiest of the three and its reading (0.6334) is the middle one.
* Two figures, not two models: `VATRenderer.FigureOfArchetype` draws the AT rifleman as Soldier and
  the Sniper as Sniper. This measures the poses those two figures are in, not a new model.
* The set costs 39 MB of git history, and that is the price of the frame that makes the gun
  measurable. Shrinking the frame puts the gun back under the gate.
