atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1 — three KneelIdle phases that move
========================================================

Why there is a round g1-g1
--------------------------
The owner threw out atrifle-look-r3-g1 in his own words: "The three sniper stills are one kneel.
Phases are 0.89, 0.89 and 0.87, so spread_sniper 0.40 is noise on a frozen man and the 0.6 bar sits
only 0.2 above it. Shoot three KneelIdle phases that move, a gap of at least 0.2, the way the prone
phases 0.95, 0.45 and 0.32 do. Keep one camera and both men in one frame at zoom 30, and put the
silhouette bar outside the worse self-spread of those three captures of the current build."

He was right, and that set's own poses.json says why. Its driver shot `sniper-{r}` straight after
writing the Sniper back onto his trench post from off the map, with no settle and no drift. That
write makes the controller play `JumpDown` — rung Trench, a once-clip that saturates at its end — so
all three stills are the same saturated JumpDown frame:

    sniper-1   clip JumpDown  phase 0.89   yaw -0.30
    sniper-2   clip JumpDown  phase 0.89   yaw -0.97
    sniper-3   clip JumpDown  phase 0.87   yaw -1.03

while the SAME man in the SAME round's pair still is `KneelIdle` at yaw 0.00 every time. So the old
`spread_sniper` 0.40 was mask noise plus three different headings on one frozen pose. The AT
rifleman's path already had a settle and a drift; the Sniper's had neither.

What changed in the capture
---------------------------
1. `settle_sniper_only`, the mirror of `settle_at_only`: after the staging write the sim runs in
   short spells and refreezes until he is back in KneelIdle, so JumpDown has finished before the
   shutter. The clip list is exactly ("KneelIdle",). KneelAimedIdle and KneelInspect are gone — they
   are other poses of the man, not other phases of one pose, and KneelAimedIdle is baked as a single
   held frame (`AnimationController` gives it `O(0.08f, ...)`).

2. The drift is a PHASE BAND, not a number of seconds, and it runs for BOTH men. Each man is driven
   to a target phase per round — 0.15, 0.45, 0.78, band ±0.06 — by running the sim in 0.15 s spells
   and re-reading the phase. The smallest pairwise gap of those targets is 0.30, against the 0.2 the
   goal asks. NOTHING is written to the world during a drift: a staging write is exactly what pinned
   the old phases.

3. Two mechanical repairs found while shooting, both written down because they cost a round each:
   * **A man off the map dies.** The sim clears the Alive flag on a slot parked at (-5000, -5000),
     and writing Hp 80 back is not enough: he returns as `DeathThrown`, stance Dead. The staging
     write now ORs the Alive flag back (`w.Flags[s] |= 1`), which also clears the controller's death
     latch; he resumes KneelIdle at his post. Suppression and Alarm are pinned to 0 in the same
     write, so no Shield beat.
   * **`Pending()` is the queue, not the file.** A shot leaves `CaptureRig`'s queue the moment it is
     dequeued — before it is posed, rendered or written. Waiting on `Pending() == 0` therefore called
     `Release()` (which puts the camera back) in the middle of the render and the still silently
     never appeared, with no `[rig]` error. The driver now waits on the PNG itself.
   * **A drift budget must cover a full loop of the SLOWER idle.** KneelIdle is a 1.7 s loop but
     ProneIdle is 4.9 s, so a 2.0 s budget stalled the AT rifleman at ProneIdle 0.54 on his way to
     0.78. It is 6.0 s of drift in the right clip, and time spent waiting out a `TrenchRoutine` beat
     (AimedIdle, StoopIdle, KneelInspect, Shield) is not counted against it.

What this set still does not cover
----------------------------------
* **The parapet halves the prone man.** At yaw 180 the AT rifleman lies behind the sandbags and the
  Sniper kneels clear of them, so the reading is "a prone man half behind bags" against "a kneeling
  man in the open". That is what the game shows from here, and it is why his contrast is 0.27-0.32
  against the Sniper's 0.80 and his mask a quarter the size. A reading of 0.77 is honest for THIS
  camera; it is not a claim about the two models side by side.
* **The rifle is not legible at zoom 30.** The 1.65 m anti-tank rifle is a few dark pixels. No
  number here isolates it, so nothing in this set says the weapon reads.
* **The two men's headings differ by 1.11 rad**, reported as `heading_diff_rad` and NOT gated: one
  camera yaw cannot be side-on to two men whose headings are snapped to their posts, and one frame
  was the owner's call. What IS gated is each man against himself across the stills of one round.
* **One seed, one ground, one time of day.** Seed 12648430, battlefield 1917, ShelledForest, night
  with braziers lit. Nothing here says the number holds on another map or in daylight.
* **Two figures, not two models.** Every infantry archetype except the sniper is drawn as the
  Soldier figure (`VATRenderer.FigureOfArchetype`), so what is measured is the Soldier figure prone
  against the Sniper figure kneeling — a pose-and-figure difference, not a per-unit model.

How to re-run it
----------------
    python docs/evidence/atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1/probe.py      # exit 0 on this set

The probe writes probe.json beside itself. It needs numpy, scipy and Pillow and reads nothing but
the fifteen PNGs, their sidecars and poses.json — no editor.

Negative control: the same probe.py run against the fifteen stills of atrifle-look-r3-g1 exits 2 and
names exactly what the owner rejected —

    FAIL: the sniper still is not KneelIdle in every round (round 1: JumpDown, round 2: JumpDown,
          round 3: JumpDown) - a different clip is a different pose, not a different phase of one
    FAIL: the sniper's KneelIdle phases [0.89, 0.89, 0.87] come within 0.000 of each other, want 0.2
    FAIL: the sniper is turned in {'sniper-1': 0.3, 'sniper-2': 0.97, 'sniper-3': 1.03} rad against
          his heading in that round's pair still, over 0.05 - that is a heading, not a pose
    FAIL: the at's ProneIdle phases [0.95, 0.45, 0.32] come within 0.130 of each other, want 0.2

so the new gates bite on the set they were written for, and the 0.2 bar also catches the AT
rifleman's old 0.13 gap.
