What the owner threw out, and what this part does
-------------------------------------------------
The previous set (docs/evidence/atrifle-look-r3-g1, commit 1eb46aa6aa) read 0.766 for the prone AT rifleman against
the Sniper at zoom 30, yaw 180. The owner's words: at that camera the prone man is "a dark lump in the sandbag gap",

His heading, which is the other half of the owner's complaint
-------------------------------------------------------------
A man's yaw cannot be written: he snaps to his post's facing, and the previous set paid 1.11 rad for it in every
reading. `AnimationController.cs:462` sets BodyYaw = atan2(step.x, step.z) whenever the shown speed is over 0.15,
and a staging teleport IS a step. So the staging ends with a one-metre hop along the Sniper's own drawn yaw: he is
written a metre back along psi, then onto the target with Speed 1, then left to settle back into ProneIdle with
Speed 0 (a settle write cannot move a heading, because the speed in it is 0). Read back per candidate in
cand-poses.json. It works: nine of the twelve candidates come back at a heading difference of 0.00 to 0.01 rad
instead of 1.11, and the three that do not (cand1 2.86, cand3 1.75, cand11 1.36) are candidates where the man ends
up on a trench post that re-snaps him.

THE FIRST HALF OF THE OWNER'S GOAL IS MET, AND MEASURED. In front of the bags at z 27-28 the prone man masks 759 to
1123 px where he masked 286 to 383 behind them, and the ratio against the kneeling man is 0.65 to 0.97 where the
owner asked for 0.5. His own box is his, not the bags': px_per_m is 25.0-25.5 from the capture's own tall_px, and
the two boxes never overlap. z 29 and z 30 are no good at any x - he drops behind the ground or out of sight
entirely (7 to 161 px, and cand7-at is flagged "men barely separate from the ground: unreadable").

THE SECOND HALF IS NOT MET, AND THAT IS WHY staging.py EXITS 2. Not one of the twelve has ZERO weather pixels inside
his box: the best is cand9 with 50 px of the 8459 px box (and 34 in the kneel's). It is not a brazier in the box -
three plates shot 7 FRAMES apart at this camera gave 1570 to 1826 px of weather over the whole frame and 0 inside
either man's box - it is the SPAN. A candidate's two plates are about 135-190 frames apart, because restaging a man
needs live frames for the presenter to move him at all, and over that span the fires flare and the lit ground under
them moves. A tighter round was shot to buy the gate with frames instead of moving it (one stepped frame per poll
instead of three, four staging writes instead of six): the span came down from 190 to 135 frames, the weather in the
box did not clear (50 px at best), and the shorter writes cost a still - cand9-at came back with men_in_frame 0,
the man not yet moved home - so the full-write version was re-shot and is what is committed here.

So the gate stands at 0 and the measurement says 25-1292. That is a question for the owner, not for this leg:
`docs/reference/decisions.md`, "Open: waiting on the owner". The default taken there is cand9, (51, 27) - the
largest mask ratio of the clean candidates at 0.811, heading difference 0.00 rad, 50 weather pixels in his box.
