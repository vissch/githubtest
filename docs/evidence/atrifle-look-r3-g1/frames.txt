What changed in probe.py
------------------------
Two levers replaced r3's hand-typed 400 px floor, both read off the set:

  * `floor` is now a fifth KIND with MEN["floor"] = 0, so floor-1/2/3 pass the same one-camera, rain-0,
    pose_error-0, no-warning, men_in_frame gate as the other twelve stills. Fifteen stills, one camera.

    accepted when its area lies between 0.2 * px_per_m**2 and 2.0 * px_per_m**2. Both bounds are reported.
  * Untouched, as the plan required: DOMINANCE 1.5, the border check, PASS_DIFF 0.6, the phase-gap gate, the

EACH MAN IN HIS OWN BOX: THE READING                          (2026-10-20, atrifle-look-r3-g1, leg 08)

Two bugs in the capture, found by the pixels
--------------------------------------------
The boxes the previous leg added (`manN_img_x/img_y/tall_px`) were both wrong on a live shot, and the
hand-computed test could not see it because it hands ManBox its own matrices:

  * THE ASPECT. The shot renders into a 1600x900 render texture, but `cam.projectionMatrix` still carries
    the aspect of this editor's game view - 482 x 797 in batch mode, aspect 0.605 against the image's 1.778.
    Every man was reported about 2.9x too far from the middle horizontally (the Sniper drawn at x 867 was
    reported at x 991). CaptureRig.Measure now builds `Matrix4x4.Perspective(fov, w/h, near, far)` itself.
  * THE GROUND. The sim keeps every man's y at 0 and the renderer lifts him onto the terrain. The box was
    projected from the sim's y, so at (45, 25) - ground 3.07 m - it sat 77 px too low and missed him
    completely: a mask inside it came back 0 to 6 px. `Measure` now adds `Map.Height.Sample(x, z)`.

Both are covered by `ManBoxFollowsTheImagesAspectAndNotTheGameViews` in CaptureRigMeasureTests, which names
the wrong aspect's answer (x 1258 for a man the image's aspect puts at x 955.9) so it cannot creep back.
The vertical was right all along: `fieldOfView` is the vertical one and does not depend on the aspect.
