The readability the critic asked for is instead plain in the stills and in the probe finding six men as an
evenly spaced row on a lit, fog-free field.

What the pre set says: the bodies already come to rest 1.18-2.14 m (median per run) from where they stood,
against a 0.08-0.29 m noise floor — so a bar at 1.5 m does not sit outside this spread. The change in part 2
has to clear the top of it, not the bottom: the throw the controller records is 4-12 m per man (logged by the
harness), and a body that travels a sixth of that is the fault the critic saw as 'still in a heap'.
