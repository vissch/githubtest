Two things carried over from r3's note, both held here too:
  * While SimHost.TimeScale is 0 the presenter does not move anyone; every staging write happens with the sim
    running, and the freeze comes last.
  * atrifle.draw is read live (Knobs.Generation), set by LookLab from a MonoBehaviour Update which keeps running
    in Play even while the SIM is frozen — SimProbe.Unit(14) was re-read after every knob flip to confirm the
    man's tick, position and yaw had not moved; they never did.
