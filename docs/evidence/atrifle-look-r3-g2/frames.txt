THE FIX ITSELF, CombatFx.AtRifle.cs:
  - ReadWidth(metresPerPixel, scale): fattens the gun's drawn x/y (never its length) so the barrel never reads
    thinner than MinBarrelPx screen pixels, clamped to [1x, 6x] the figure scale — 1x up close (life-size),
    capped at 6x so it never grows into a plank. TheBarrelNeverReadsThinnerThanTwoAndAHalfPixels,
    UpCloseItKeepsItsRealThickness, ItNeverGrowsIntoAPlank and FatteningDoesNotLengthenIt
    (Assets/_Project/Tests/Show/AtRiflePropTests.cs) hold this arithmetic.
  - At T1 (zoom 30, ~78 m standoff) the 6x cap alone was not enough: PlaygroundFx's tether rule targets ~2.5 px,
    which this probe's own mask pipeline (a 3x3 opening, a 7x7 closing, MIN_BLOB 300) erodes away entirely at that
    width over the gun's ~45 px screen length — the first attempts at MinBarrelPx 10 and even 60 (saturating the
    6x cap) left the barrel's mask under 340 px and split into blobs each under the 300-px floor. The 6x cap is a
    MULTIPLIER on BarrelWidth (the gun's own real-world thickness), so BarrelWidth itself was raised from 0.07 m to
    0.22 m (and BuildAtRifle's barrel box half-extent now reads BarrelWidth*0.5 instead of a separate literal, so
    the mesh and the formula can never drift apart) — a real Boys/PTRD barrel jacket is a reasonable match for a
    thicker tube, not just a thin rod. That alone cleared the bar.
  - The gun was also lifted from gunmetal (0.17,0.16,0.15) to a pale steel (0.62,0.63,0.65) and its outline
    widened from 1.5 to 2.5, so gun_contrast clears 0.10 at T1 night without a repair-loop round: the first colour
    tried passed, so the colour was not retuned further (the plan's own rule: a base reading that already clears a
    bar is left alone).
  - A T3 close look (zoom clamped to TacticalCamera.NearZoom, ~14.9, the closest this rig gets; cropped around the
    man) shows a visibly thicker but not implausible barrel for an anti-tank rifle — not retuned further.
