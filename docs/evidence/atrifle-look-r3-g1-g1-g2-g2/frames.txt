atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2-g2 — the same nine stills, scaled to one height before the IoU
=========================================================================================

Why there is a round g1-g1-g2-g2
---------------------------------
The owner's words: "The three readings are 0.763, 0.785 and 0.785. That is the size gap of a
272-296 px helmet against a 1136-1264 px kneel after centering with no scale, and the 0.6 bar sits
0.16 under a cluster that only moves 0.022. Scale both masks to one height before the IoU, and set
the bar just outside that spread. The sniper's 0.235 spread is the lamp (127 noise px in round 3),
not a kneel a player can see change. Start from lane/show/atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2's tip. Bar outside
the spread of three captures of the current build."

Nothing about the game changes this round; only the measure does. No Unity, no capture, no editor:
this folder holds no PNGs or JSONs of its own. It re-measures the nine stills committed at
f67e6b76ea under `docs/evidence/atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2/` - the same pair-{r}/empty-{r}/floor-{r}
frames, the same poses.json, every gate upstream of the reading (one frame, one camera, the boxes
from the capture's own projection, the weather subtraction, the phase and heading gates) unchanged.
That proves the move from 0.763 to 0.3119 is the scale normalization and not another sim instant.

What changed in the measure
----------------------------
`atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2`'s probe centred each mask on its own centroid - translation only, no
scale, no rotation - before the IoU. But the AT rifleman's mask is 17-18 px tall (272-296 px, a
crouch behind the parapet) and the Sniper's is 39-43 px tall (1136-1264 px, a kneel standing clear
of it): three quarters of the 0.76-0.78 reading was that size gap, not shape.

This round adds exactly one step before the IoU: each mask is cropped to its own bounding box,
resized (bilinear) so its height is NORM_HEIGHT_PX (128), re-thresholded at > 0.5, then centred on
a common canvas by its own centroid, same as before. `spread()` runs through the same scaled `diff`,
so the self-spreads are scale-free too.

    round   old reading (no scale)   new reading (scaled to one height)
    1       0.7628                   0.3119
    2       0.7848                   0.4124
    3       0.7847                   0.3697

The reading is resolution-free: NORM_HEIGHT_PX 64 / 128 / 256 give round 1 readings of
0.3192 / 0.3119 / 0.3118 - the resample's own edge pixels move the number by under 0.01, so 128 was
not a number the reading happened to need.

THE BAR
-------
PASS_DIFF moves from 0.6 to 0.20. The 0.6 lineage (unchanged since 13d1158177) ends here: every
round back to that commit compared UNSCALED masks, and a scaled reading is not comparable to an
unscaled one, so no round before this one is a baseline for 0.20.

Derivation, from the three scaled readings of this build: 0.3119 / 0.4124 / 0.3697. Their spread
(max - min) is 0.1005. 0.3119 - 0.1005 = 0.2114, snapped down to the nearest 0.05, is 0.20 - a
reading has to fall a whole capture-to-capture spread below the worst of three captures before it
fails, so the bar sits just outside the spread the owner asked for, not inside it.

Pass needs min(readings) >= PASS_DIFF AND max(spread_readings, spread_at) < PASS_DIFF.

`spread_sniper` is reported and NOT gated. It is 0.2782 here (it was 0.2353 under the old, unscaled
measure - also not the 0.235 the owner's message quoted from memory, but the same order and the same
round). The owner ruled it the lamp (127 noise px in round 3's Sniper box, see
`noise_in_box_px_sniper_pair-3` in `atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2/probe.json`), and the pixels do not
settle that call either way: the Sniper's KneelIdle phases in this set are 0.65 / 0.25 / 0.98, and
the three pairwise self-diffs of his SCALED masks are 0.164 (round 1-2), 0.183 (1-3) and 0.278
(2-3) - even the two rounds that do NOT touch round 3's noisy box differ by 0.16. A man read at
three live phases of a kneel is a pose difference, not the repeatability of the measure, so it is
reported (`spread_sniper`, `spread_sniper_above_bar` true) and left out of the pass/fail rather than
dropped.

What this set still does not cover (carried over from atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2, plus two new lines)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* **The parapet halves the prone man.** At yaw 180 the AT rifleman lies behind the sandbags and the
  Sniper kneels clear of them, so the reading is "a prone man half behind bags" against "a kneeling
  man in the open". That is what the game shows from here, and it is why his mask is a quarter the
  size of the Sniper's.
* **The rifle is not legible at zoom 30.** The 1.65 m anti-tank rifle is a few dark pixels. No
  number here isolates it, so nothing in this set says the weapon reads.
* **The two men's headings differ by 1.11 rad**, reported as `heading_diff_rad` and NOT gated: one
  camera yaw cannot be side-on to two men whose headings are snapped to their posts. What IS gated
  is each man against himself across the three pair stills.
* **One seed, one ground, one time of day.** Seed 12648430, battlefield 1917, ShelledForest, night
  with braziers lit. Nothing here says the number holds on another map or in daylight.
* **Two figures, not two models.** Every infantry archetype except the sniper is drawn as the
  Soldier figure, so what is measured is the Soldier figure prone against the Sniper figure
  kneeling - a pose-and-figure difference, not a per-unit model.
* **Three poses are three poses, not the clip.** Each man was shot at three spaced phases of ONE
  idle loop. That is a floor for the measure, not a survey of everything he does.
* **NEW: the reading is of the same order as the Sniper's own phase-to-phase change.** 0.31-0.41
  against the Sniper's 0.16-0.28 self-spread says "a prone silhouette differs from a kneeling one by
  about a third of their union", not "they are unmistakable" - the gated bar (0.20) is well clear
  of both, but a reader should not hear 0.31 and picture two shapes that share almost nothing.
* **NEW: scaling to one height removes the size gap, not the pose.** A reader who wants "is the AT
  rifleman recognizably smaller/lower than the Sniper" has to look at `mask_m2_*` in
  `atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2/probe.json` (0.43-0.47 m2 against 1.81-2.02 m2) - this round's number no
  longer carries that difference at all.

How to re-run it
----------------
    python docs/evidence/atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2-g2/probe.py              # exit 0 on this set
    python docs/evidence/atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2-g2/probe.py --selftest   # the scaling arithmetic,
                                                                            # no stills

The probe reads the nine stills and poses.json from `../atrifle-look-r3-g1-g1-g2/` (no copies here)
and writes `probe.json` beside itself. It needs numpy, scipy and Pillow. `probe.py <another folder>`
measures another set and prints the report WITHOUT writing probe.json.
