## Owner decisions (2026-09-26, `reference/decisions.md`)
Both lanes in one session (seams first, alone); man-made props true to the soldier while machines stay giant;
death physics are VAT launched bodies (no ragdolls); mines are laid by a sapper unit during the match (no
preparation phase). Assumed by default: the beam is an off-map line-targeted sweep; Iron and Brass are the playable
factions; explosions batches B/C and the destruction-v2 LOD/scar batches stay parked.

## Rules this work runs under
- Seams (`SimWorld` fields and `Hash()`, `SimConfig`, command meanings, the events contract, archetype ids, asmdef
  lists, `FormatVersion`) land as their own commit, alone, first. `Hash()` appends at the end. Replay versions are
  assigned at landing time in landing order (units-meta v5/v6 first if it lands first; then upgrades, deaths,
  abilities, beam, sapper). Each seam: a `02-contracts.md` row, a `DeterminismReplayTests` case, hash coverage.
- No editor opens under ~4 GB free (`reference/workflow.md` section 2). The loop is the offline Roslyn compile, then
  the batch gate with the editor closed. Bakes, scene builds and Play checks wait for an editor session.
- New code goes in new files or partials (`CombatFx`, `AnimationController`, `VATRenderer`, `BattlefieldComposer`
  are at or past the 1,000-line split point). Every new test, hook, tool and flag is routed in `reference/` or
  `validate.py` fails.

## Phase 4: deaths
**SIM-A seam.** `DeathCause { Blast = -1, Gas = -2, Crush = -3 (reserved), Burning = -4, Beam = -5 }` in the Death
event's `b` (killer slot when >= 0); `dir` = knock direction (blast: `dir.y = 1`), `scalar` = knock speed. `Blast`
computes the knock for the dead too. `BurningSystem` (order 725) with hashed `AlightUntil`, burning cells, 12 hp/s,
`UnitAlight` events, `Despawn(..., Burning)`.
**SHOW.** Six clips (`DeathBackHeadshot`, `DeathWalking2`, `DeathStagger`, `DeathGas`, `DeathCrushed`,
`DeathBurning`) and one bake. `AnimationController.Death.cs` (partial): death latch, a 256-record death ring read
by `CombatFx.Deaths.cs` (closes the slot-reuse hazard), density within 4 m / 12 ticks scaling the throw (`1 + 0.35 *
min(n, 5)`), tumble encoded in `VatInstance.Tint` (32 pitch steps), pile-up from a 2 m body-count grid in
`VATRenderer.Fallen.cs`, gibs scaled by density. Flame death: `VatPad` bits 22-23 = char level, `VAT_URP` blackens
and embers (no discard), a smoulder list re-emits smoke cards 8 s, charred bodies shrink as they sink.

## Phases 1-3: environment
**Phase 1.** One soldier unit (SU) = `FigureMetrics.HeightM` (1.78 m bake x `UnitScale`, 2.0 m); the readability
grow is a men-only exception. `Presentation/Terrain/AssetScaleTable.cs`: `ScaleClass { Strict, Structure, Organic,
Machine }` with bounds per module key (imported props by `Module.Name`, procedural kit by field name, buildings,
vehicles); `AssetScaleReport.Measure` generates the real field with no scene and applies looks exactly
(`PropLayout.Style` extracted from `BattlefieldProps.Styled`); `AssetScaleTests` fails a Strict/Structure anomaly;
`Editor/AssetScaleAudit.cs` writes `docs/reference/asset-scale.md` by menu or `-executeMethod`. Enforcement clamps
in `BattlefieldProps.Emit` and `Placement` (not `Styled`, which returns early without a look) and `Restyle` keeps
`Module.Size` consistent. `Tools/looks.py` patches the looks that were learned when men were 2.67 m: Sandbag 2.99
-> 1.20, FieldGun 3.50 x 2.83 x 5.58 -> 1.10 x 0.89 x 1.75, ArmouredStand -> 1.41, SodShelterRuin -> 1.30, MGNest
-> 0.85, SplitStump/FallenLog become multipliers so the generator's sizes return. Organic modules get wider ranges
and a per-instance tint; vehicles are audit rows only.
**Phase 2.** `BattlefieldComposer` is split into partials by step, then `Presentation/Terrain/Scatter/` adds fields
built once per map at 2 m (Traffic from trench/link cells, corridors between opposing links through wire gaps, the
supply road and the cart-rut band; Vertical from obstructions the composer records as it emits; Patch from seeded
noise; Open from nav layers, banks and structure footprints; winter and coast gates) and layers (grass on two
unnamed micro/small modules with page caps, flowers only inside grass, camp kit, crates, lanterns and wall debris
only on trench cells, dugout sites and the rear band). Per-instance tint via an `_INSTTINT` Toon variant kept by a
ShaderKeep material. The scatter step runs after sites, houses and landmarks; recomposition re-emits cached
placements filtered by fresh craters and `Suppress`. `ScatterRulesTests` on a hand-built field.
**Phase 3.** The 2 m lining panels already break; the gap is a Damaged state: damaged variants of walls, bags and
floors from `BuildTrenchVariants`, `TrenchSectionState` (damaged at 50 %, heavy ordnance = radius >= 6 or power >= 1
in the inner half of its reach shatters outright), shared identity through `Key(rule.Intact ?? module)`,
`BattlefieldProps.Swap`/`Replace`, a per-strike piece budget, `DebrisRenderer.ZoomShare` and a 12 s `Life` for
lining pieces, `FrameBudget` coverage of prop draws. `TrenchSectionTests`. About 20 developer-days for the three.

## Order and size
SIM-A and Phase 1 first (no dependencies), then the death batches, scatter and trench, SIM-B/C with the targeting
UI and effects, the meta scenes and screens; SIM-D, the upgrades seam and the staging pool swap wait for
`lane/show/units-meta`. About 60 developer-days on the combat and meta halves before the environment half.
