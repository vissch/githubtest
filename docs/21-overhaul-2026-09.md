## Owner decisions (2026-09-26, `reference/decisions.md`)
Both lanes in one session (seams first, alone); man-made props true to the soldier while machines stay giant;
death physics are VAT launched bodies (no ragdolls); mines are laid by a sapper unit during the match (no
preparation phase). Assumed by default: the beam is an off-map line-targeted sweep; Iron and Brass are the playable
factions; explosions batches B/C and the destruction-v2 LOD/scar batches stay parked.

## Rules this work runs under
- Seams (`SimWorld` fields and `Hash()`, `SimConfig`, command meanings, the events contract, archetype ids, asmdef
  lists, `FormatVersion`) land as their own commit, alone, first. `Hash()` appends at the end. Replay versions are
  assigned at landing time in landing order (units-meta v5/v6 first if it lands first; then upgrades, deaths,
  abilities, beam, sapper). Each seam: a `02-contracts.md` row, a `DeterminismReplayTests` case, hash coverage.
- No editor opens under ~4 GB free (`reference/workflow.md` section 2). The loop is the offline Roslyn compile, then
  the batch gate with the editor closed. Bakes, scene builds and Play checks wait for an editor session.
- New code goes in new files or partials (`CombatFx`, `AnimationController`, `VATRenderer`, `BattlefieldComposer`
  are at or past the 1,000-line split point). Every new test, hook, tool and flag is routed in `reference/` or
  `validate.py` fails.

## Phase 4: deaths
**SIM-A seam.** `DeathCause { Blast = -1, Gas = -2, Crush = -3 (reserved), Burning = -4, Beam = -5 }` in the Death
event's `b` (killer slot when >= 0); `dir` = knock direction (blast: `dir.y = 1`), `scalar` = knock speed. `Blast`
computes the knock for the dead too. `BurningSystem` (order 725) with hashed `AlightUntil`, burning cells, 12 hp/s,
`UnitAlight` events, `Despawn(..., Burning)`.
**SHOW.** Six clips (`DeathBackHeadshot`, `DeathWalking2`, `DeathStagger`, `DeathGas`, `DeathCrushed`,
`DeathBurning`) and one bake. `AnimationController.Death.cs` (partial): death latch, a 256-record death ring read
by `CombatFx.Deaths.cs` (closes the slot-reuse hazard), density within 4 m / 12 ticks scaling the throw (`1 + 0.35 *
min(n, 5)`), tumble encoded in `VatInstance.Tint` (32 pitch steps), pile-up from a 2 m body-count grid in
`VATRenderer.Fallen.cs`, gibs scaled by density. Flame death: `VatPad` bits 22-23 = char level, `VAT_URP` blackens
and embers (no discard), a smoulder list re-emits smoke cards 8 s, charred bodies shrink as they sink.

## Phase 5: abilities
**SIM-B seam.** `AbilityArgs.Pack(heading, pattern, length)` in `SupportFire.B`; `ScheduledPayload` gains `Dir`,
`Radius`, `Kind`; `AbilitySlots` 16. StrafeRun (id 10): 24 light impacts along a corridor. HE patterns disc/line/box;
CreepingBarrage (id 2): 10 lifts of 4 shells stepping 6 m every 80 ticks, own men safe 15 m behind. SmokeScreen
(id 6): a second grid in `GasSmokeSystem`, `SmokeLos.MetresThrough` cuts sight at 12.5 m and accuracy 8 %/m.
Creeping gas: chlorine pattern 1, eight sources stepping 8 m.
**SIM-C seam.** Beam (id 11): `BeamSystem` (order 723), hashed active beams, a segment sweep at 240 dps (x0.7 in a
trench), 1,200 dps to hulls, lights men, scorches props through `BlastShape.Beam` impacts.
**SIM-D seam (after units-meta).** `InfantryArchetype.Sapper` in both faction pools with mine and wire charges;
`UnitAbilityId.LayMine/LayTripwire` consumed by `SpecialAbilitiesSystem`; `MineSystem` (order 1130): hashed mines
and tripwires, enemy-only triggers, HE clearing, track hits.
**SHOW.** `UI/Abilities/AbilityTargeting.cs` replaces the `TestPanel` arm path (click, drag-a-line, pattern
sub-buttons, `SceneHooks.AimPreview` drawn by `CombatFx.Aim.cs`); six support cards; `StrafeFx` (biplane flyover),
`BeamFx` + `TW/Beam (URP)`, `MineFx`, `CombatFx.Smoke.cs`; sapper LAY buttons on the selection panel.

## Phase 6: Home Front and the campaign map
`UI/Campaign/CampaignDefinition` (six country nodes, 3-4 ordered `MissionCard`s each, prerequisites, gold
25/40/75), `FactionBuildings` (a code table: building stages 40/80/150 gold cap the tiers they sell; unit tracks at
15/20/40/60/80/100; global lines for armour, silver, income, ability cooldown/cost, veteran survival),
`CampaignProfile` + `ProfileStore` (`profile.json`, versioned like settings). SIM seam after units-meta: `SimConfig.
UpgradesA/B` (+5 % per tier on hp, damage, accuracy keyed by faction pool index; armour +2 mm per tier) and
`AbilityMaskA/B`, applied at `Spawn`, in `DirectFire`, `TankGunnery`, `VehicleModules`, `OffMapAbilitySystem`;
hashed; in the replay header. New assembly `TW.Presentation.Meta` (`HomeFrontDiorama` on `HouseKit` chunk masks
for visible stages, chimney smoke, lamps; `StrategicMapView` on a generated continent mesh with pins, a front-line
ribbon and fog; `MetaCamera`), driven by `TW.UI` through `IHomeFrontView`/`IStrategicMapView` in
`Presentation/Core`. Scenes `HomeFront` and `StrategicMap` built by `ShellAssetsBuilder.BuildMenuScenes`; screens
HomeFront, StrategicMap, Staging (battalion slots from the faction pool, tiers, up to two ability picks; DEPLOY
writes loadout, upgrades and mask into the launch request), Debrief awards gold, Main Menu gains CAMPAIGN and HOME
FRONT.
