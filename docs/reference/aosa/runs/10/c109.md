# C109: fx.columnPlay, the night column cut before its arc frames (cycle 10)

The knob: fx.columnPlay cuts the old dry Column card on a moonlit field at Cut x its 1.8 s life. The frames run on the old clock, and the card fades over the last 35% of its shortened life. 1 = today. Code: runs/10/C109.patch.

Runs: barrage, ShelledForest night.

- 64 frames from tick 140: p0 (default), p4 (0.4), p5 (0.5), p4c (0.4 + fx.columnCap=1), p5c (0.5 + cap).
- 32 frames from tick 180: p0t, p4ct, p5ct.

p0 is identical to k0, frame for frame, in both images and arc pixels. The detector is C108's (runs/10/c108.md), unchanged.

The critic's notes:

- The arcs read as "muddy water jets or flame, not soil" (smooth, banded, see-through ribbons with no grit). The side without them sells earth through its dark clods.
- Where the arcs cross the top-right trench (f0), the helmets are muddier, costing about 1 point.
- The arcs add "a little motion but no violence". The weight comes from the bursts, the debris and the smoke. The weight losses (6 -> 5) are the centre-burst frames f50/f56, where the big fountain vanished.

Rule 6: both sets pass.

- Earth went up in 12 of 12 pairs.
- Men were never down, in any pair or on average.
- Weight stayed above the -0.5 floor (-0.17 for p4c, -0.33 for p5c).

## Decision

Default candidate: p4c = fx.columnPlay=0.4 with fx.columnCap=1. It has the larger earth gain and the smaller weight cost, and removes 70% of the arc px (87% late). Its heave change is small (0.14% of pixels in f12-25).

Fallback: p5c (fx.columnPlay=0.5 with fx.columnCap=1). It keeps the heave practically bit-identical, at weight -0.33.

fx.columnBurstLit stays 1 (inert, C108).

This is one critic. Before landing it, a second critic or the whole default set (a0050's lesson) is advisable.
