# C73: white-out in night streak bundles (cycle 9)

Evidence: `9/c71f-1.png` and `.f1`..`.f31`, 32 consecutive held-clock frames at tick 100, T1 standard view, night,
ShelledForest, no HUD, release player at 14ccd6f (after C22 and C48). No new bench. Script: `c73.py` in this
folder (Pillow, numpy, scipy); `python c73.py --debug DIR` writes a mask overlay per frame.

## What the frames show

- The core of every tracer is opaque unlit (3.0, 2.7, 2.3) and lands as pure 255 white. A raw count of luma >= 0.98
  in a bundle is therefore mostly core: up to 14,135 px in one bundle (f29), and >= 50 in every frame.
- Where many lanes converge (by the pillbox, in the left fan) the cores overlap into solid white bands up to ~24 px
  across (f8 x 1200, y 331-354). White wider than one core (it survives a 7x7 opening): up to 3,432 px in one bundle
  (f28), >= 50 in 27 of 32 frames, and 77% of it is pure 255.
- The halos never make a white pixel. Bundle pixels 2 px or more from any white (halo and ground only): 0 px at luma
  >= 0.98 in all 32 frames, max luma 0.9796. Stacked green halos clip G and keep R low (f28 and f6: median 0.21,
  99th percentile 0.41-0.43, e.g. (85, 255, 150)); the few brighter ones, up to (215, 255, 171) with luma ~0.965, sit
  where red lanes cross or beside the pillbox muzzle flash. The 1 px gaps between stacked cores stay green: each
  white band has a hard edge onto R ~0.27 halo.
- G-R at the green halo edge (1-2 px outside the white): mean 0.354 over the 32 frames (per frame 0.27-0.42, lowest
  single bundle 0.09); beside the widest slab, mean 0.38. R-G at the red edge: 0.18-0.52. The cycle 2 reading of
  G-R ~0 at the halo edge does not hold after C22 and C48.

## Decision

C74 closes. The card's gate (some frame keeps >= 50 white px in a bundle) is met only by core white, which a Max
blend on the halo materials cannot touch. C74's own discriminating still ("the bundle's white px must fall at the
knob") would fail by construction, and its hue target (G-R > 0.2 at the halo edge) already holds at 0.35. If a
critic reads the converging white bands as a white-out, the lever is the core (its width or its overlap), not the
halo blend.
