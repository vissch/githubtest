# Melee and the range cut (lane/sim/melee, replay v23 and v24)

**For:** every SHOW lane that draws men or machines, and any SIM lane that bumps `FormatVersion`.

What the sim does now (the owner's words and answers: `decisions.md`, four rows of 2026-09-28; the contract: the v23
and v24 rows of `docs/02-contracts.md`):
- **Every attack reach is 0.8 of its design** (`CombatTables.RangeScale`). The HUD tooltips in `UnitLook.cs` still quote
  the old metres, and `HudBindTests` / `HudTextTests` fail until they read x0.8.
- **Men fight hand to hand.** A man on foot charges an enemy man within 8 m (`EngageClose`, `UnitFlags.Melee`). Within
  2.5 m both are in `Stance.Melee` (`MovementSystem.EngageMelee`), facing each other, with blows about every 1.2 s:
  `MeleeBlow`, where `dir.y` is the style (0 stab, 1 rifle butt, 2 smash, 3 fists) and scalar is the damage (0 blocked,
  -1 missed). A landed blow also sends the ordinary `Hit`.
- **Fists men throw their weapon down.** Assault troops, officers, sappers, medics, shield, jetpack and flame men
  (`MeleeSystem.KeepsRifle`) send `WeaponDropped` with `dir` as the throw, and carry `UnitFlags.Disarmed` until
  `WeaponPickedUp`.
- **Crabs pounce.** A walker with claws sends `PounceCrouched` (0.6 s, then 0.5 s in the air to `pos`, with
  `UnitFlags.Pouncing` set), then `PounceLanded` (scalar is the radius). The men under it get `Hit` and `Death` with the
  crab as their killer.

What to do on a SHOW lane (tw3d-board item `melee`, stage `show`):
- Draw `Stance.Melee` and the four already-baked melee clips (`MeleeStab`, `MeleePunch`, `MeleeSmash`, `MeleeBlock`).
- Add the rows for the five new events to `GymCatalogue.EventHow` (`GymCatalogueTests` fails without them).
- Update the `UnitLook` ranges.

New clips (a bare-fisted punch, throwing the rifle down, picking it up) are for the batched bake (stage `clips`).

What to do on a SIM lane: `origin/lane/sim/wreck-decay` also claims replay v18. Whichever lane lands second
renumbers, and reruns the determinism and replay tests after its rebase.
