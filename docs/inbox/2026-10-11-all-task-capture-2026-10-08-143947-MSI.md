## What he saw
F10 in PROVING GROUND on 2026-10-08, tick 13755, zoom 18.15: "there is huge parts that spawned from the units".
Large pale shapes lying among a dense melee.

## Why
A man's parts were thrown at `FigureScale()` = `UnitScale 1.125 x Grow(zoom)` (up to x4) times
`GibPlan.PartScale` 1.8, and `DebrisRenderer.Throw` wrote that size into the 96-byte record **once**. Living and
fallen men are redrawn at the zoom of the frame (`VATRenderer`). So a leg thrown while the camera was out at 96
kept a scale of 8.1 — a 7 m leg — for the 30-60 s it lay there, beside men back at 1.125.

## The change (commit `f0c70d5`, SHOW only, no sim and no seam)
`CombatFx` now throws a man's parts at `BodyScale()` (his life size, no zoom in it), and `DebrisRenderer` carries
the men's grow of this frame as `PartGrow`, pushed per pool as the shader's `_Grow`. Positions still use the drawn
figure scale, so nothing moved. The shared pools (Clod, Rubble) never grow. The record is unchanged at 96 bytes.
`DebrisTests` pins it: a part thrown at zoom 96 and drawn at 18 is the same size as one thrown at 18.

## The pictures

staged until its men have finished dying, then put back, so the strip and the aftermath are filmed at the usual
zoom either way. Unset = today's behaviour.
